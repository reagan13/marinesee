# Glossary

Wording follows the study’s Definition of Terms unless noted.

## 3D visualization

Interactive three-dimensional representations of seagrass species inside the mobile app (rotate, zoom, inspect). Not VR or AR.

## Conservation status

Educational statement about protection need or habitat pressure for a species. In this app it is **profile text**, not a live IUCN API.

## Digital library

Searchable / browsable archive of localized species information, reference imagery, and conservation notes for Mati City seagrass.

## Image recognition

Automatic assignment of a user photo to one of the nine species. **IMPLEMENTATION:** the SVM module. See the conflict note in `00-study-review.md`.

## MarineSee

The product name. Repo folder is `seagrass`.

## Seagrass

Underwater flowering plants in Mati’s coastal waters. Not seaweeds or algae. Only the nine listed species are in scope.

## Support Vector Machine (SVM)

Supervised classifier used on **extracted** color, texture, and shape features to label a photo as one of the nine species.

## Three-dimensional (3D) model

A digital object with height, width, and depth that stands in for a species in the viewer. Authored in Blender per the paper.
