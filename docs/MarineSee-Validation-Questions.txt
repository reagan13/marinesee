DOMAIN 1: FUNCTIONAL SUITABILITY

Functional completeness
1. MarineSee lets me take a seagrass photo with the phone camera or choose a photo already saved on the phone.
2. MarineSee has a library I can browse that contains the nine seagrass species documented in Mati City.
3. From an identification result, I can open that species' profile and its 3D view.

Functional correctness
4. MarineSee shows the name of the identified species and a confidence score for that result.
5. When MarineSee is not confident in the result, it tells me to take the photo again.

Functional appropriateness
6. Each species profile shows the scientific name, common name, botanical characteristics, ecological role, conservation status, and where it grows in Mati.
7. I can rotate and zoom the 3D model so I can look at the species more closely.

DOMAIN 2: USABILITY

Appropriateness recognizability
1. It is easy to understand that MarineSee is for identifying and learning the seagrass species of Mati City.

Learnability
2. After a short explanation, I can identify a photo and open the library without someone guiding each step.

Operability
3. The camera, upload, library, and 3D controls are easy to use on a phone.

User error protection
4. The message that tells me to retake a photo is clear.

User interface aesthetics
5. The text, colors, and layout are clear and comfortable to read on a phone screen.

Accessibility
6. The labels and buttons are large enough to read and tap without difficulty.

DOMAIN 3: PERFORMANCE EFFICIENCY

Time behavior
1. MarineSee shows the identification result within about five seconds.
2. A species profile opens without a long wait.
3. Moving from the library to a species profile and its 3D view does not take long.

Resource utilization
4. I can rotate and zoom a 3D model without the screen freezing or lagging.
5. Opening several species in one session does not make the app lag.

DOMAIN 4: RELIABILITY

Maturity
1. I can identify a photo and open a species profile without the app closing or crashing.
2. I can identify another photo after the first one without the app crashing.

Availability
3. When I open MarineSee, I can use the library and the identify feature.

Fault tolerance
4. If an identification does not finish, I can try again without the app shutting down.

Recoverability
5. After I close MarineSee and open it again, the species library is still there.
6. If I leave a species profile and open it again, the same information is still shown.

DOMAIN 5: COMPATIBILITY

Co-existence
1. I can identify a photo and open the library while other apps stay installed on the same phone.
2. I do not have to uninstall other apps before MarineSee will open.

DOMAIN 6: PORTABILITY

Adaptability
1. The identify, library, and profile screens can still be read and used on Android phones with different screen sizes.
2. MarineSee runs on a phone using Android 8.0 or a newer version.

Installability
3. MarineSee can be installed on a second Android phone that meets the device requirements, without extra tools.
